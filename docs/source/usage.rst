=====
Usage
=====

Resources
---------

-   A paper describing the methods developed and implemented in Algodif is
    under review :cite:`Vo:2026`.
-   Examples of how to use the package are in the "/examples" folder at the
    `github page <https://github.com/algodif/algodif/tree/main/examples>`__:

    +   `calibrate_energy_single_strip.py <https://github.com/algodif/algodif/blob/main/examples/calibrate_energy_single_strip.py>`__:
        energy calibration of a single detector strip.
    +   `calibrate_energy_full.py <https://github.com/algodif/algodif/blob/main/examples/calibrate_energy_full.py>`__:
        energy calibration of all detector strips.
    +   `calculate_energy_resolution.py <https://github.com/algodif/algodif/blob/main/examples/calculate_energy_resolution.py>`__:
        estimation of the energy resolution of the detector.
    +   `q_calibration_single_strip.py <https://github.com/algodif/algodif/blob/main/examples/q_calibration_single_strip.py>`__,
        `q_calibration_single_strip_direct.py <https://github.com/algodif/algodif/blob/main/examples/q_calibration_single_strip_direct.py>`__:
        q-calibration of a single detector strip.
    +   `q_calibration_full.py <https://github.com/algodif/algodif/blob/main/examples/q_calibration_full.py>`__,
        `q_calibration_full_direct.py <https://github.com/algodif/algodif/blob/main/examples/q_calibration_full_direct.py>`__:
        q-calibration of all detector strips.

-   GUIs/CLIs for processing and visualizing EDDT data are in the
    `GUIs_CLIs <https://github.com/algodif/algodif/tree/main/GUIs_CLIs>`__ folder.
